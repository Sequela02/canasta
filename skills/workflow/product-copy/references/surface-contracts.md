# Surface Contracts

Use these contracts to classify the page. They identify different jobs, not mandatory layouts.

## Homepage

Orients mixed-intent visitors to the organization or product category. It normally needs to establish what the organization is, who it helps, what it can credibly do, and where different visitor intents should continue. It may introduce several offers, products, or proof types without resolving every detail.

## Campaign landing

Supports one acquisition context, audience, offer, and next action. Preserve message continuity with the source campaign. Remove navigation or secondary paths only when doing so improves that contract and does not conceal material information.

## Service page

Helps a buyer assess fit for a specific capability. Clarify the problem boundary, responsibility owned, working relationship, relevant evidence, exclusions, and next qualification step. Do not imply a fixed package when delivery is genuinely bespoke.

## Product marketing page

Explains a product's problem, operating model, differentiated capability, proof, and adoption path. Product UI must come from the product or an explicitly labeled concept, never generated imagery that impersonates a current interface.

## Portfolio or case-study index

Lets visitors evaluate the relevance and credibility of prior work. Organize by the questions buyers use to judge fit, not merely chronology or visual similarity. Respect authorization limits on client identity and outcomes.

## Documentation or public writing surface

Optimizes findability, comprehension, and continuation into the relevant task. It is not a marketing landing merely because it is public. Prefer information scent, clear hierarchy, and relevant next documentation over repeated conversion modules.

## Application surface

An authenticated or transactional workflow is outside page architecture's primary scope. Its hierarchy follows task completion, state, permissions, and error recovery rather than a marketing argument. Use the project's interface-quality guidance and the project's implementation guidance instead. If the optional stack
group is installed and the project uses React, `react-engineering` can help.

## Classification check

When multiple contracts appear to apply, identify the dominant visitor job. Split the surface or document the compromise when combining them would create competing primary actions or incompatible evidence needs.
