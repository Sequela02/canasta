# Evaluation Rubric

Score current and candidate systems independently and blind to arm. Do not reward similarity to one brand or a preferred section sequence.

## Routing

- 2: Correct skill applies to within-page marketing argument; application, documentation-navigation, visual-style, and site-architecture work stay with their owners.
- 1: Correct skill applies with unnecessary context or one boundary leak.
- 0: Skill is missed, implicitly activated during the canary, or applied to an out-of-scope surface.

## Contract, relationships, and sequence

- 2: Surface, audience, page job, primary action, dependencies, reading order, chapter weight, and content relationships are explicit; each chapter adds a new fact.
- 1: Mostly coherent with one material ambiguity or stock chapter.
- 0: Uses a generic section checklist, prescribes signature geometry, or introduces information out of dependency order.

## Evidence integrity and placement

- 2: Material claims are bound to authorized evidence, proof is placed where it qualifies the decision, and unknowns remain blocked or omitted. A source index may coexist with local qualification when it serves retrieval.
- 1: Evidence is acknowledged but weakly connected, too remote, or incompletely scoped.
- 0: Claims, proof, pricing, outcomes, commitments, or conversion predictions are invented.

## Counterexamples and restraint

- 2: FAQs, cards, repeated comparison fields, collections, timelines, and reference structures remain conditional on their information job; valid repetition is protected.
- 1: Includes unnecessary structure without material harm.
- 0: Fills quotas, duplicates the page, or forces novelty that weakens comparison or retrieval.

## Handoff verification

- 2: Heading-only argument, CTA concept, lexical review, multilingual equivalence, evidence owner, relationship handoff, and unresolved dependencies are checked.
- 1: Some verification is present.
- 0: Output is only a section list or silently fills incomplete inputs.

Promotion requires held-out, multi-arm improvement without degrading routing, non-marketing negatives, or valid counterexamples.
