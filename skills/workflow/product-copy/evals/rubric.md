# Evaluation Rubric

Score candidate outputs without revealing the arm. Evaluate voice against repository and audience evidence, never a preferred generic tone.

## Routing and authority

- 2: Applies to product communication, defers strategy, architecture, brand, legal, locale, and factual authority correctly, and rejects out-of-scope documents.
- 1: Correct task with minor responsibility leakage.
- 0: Imports another brand, invents authority, or applies to the wrong work.

## Evidence and audience vocabulary

- 2: Distinguishes product behavior, audience wording, measured evidence, approved quotation, and guarantee authority. Missing facts remain blocked.
- 1: Sources are acknowledged but one claim or vocabulary use is weakly bounded.
- 0: Anecdotes become prevalence claims, implementation becomes outcome, or unsupported guarantees are implied.

## Usefulness, literal meaning, and technical depth

- 2: Copy is concrete, actionable, literally understandable, and as technical as the audience's decision requires. Technical facts are connected to consequences without being diluted into generic benefits.
- 1: Understandable but generic, overexplained, underexplained, or weakly prioritized.
- 0: Hype, ambiguity, technical theater, or oversimplification obscures meaning.

## Voice range and AI-pattern diagnosis

- 2: Formality, energy, rhythm, humor, and density come from the brand and surface. Apparent AI patterns are changed only when they are empty or transferable; truthful triads, contrast, repetition, or editorial language may remain.
- 1: Voice is coherent but drifts toward a default terse or conversational register.
- 0: Flattens brands into one voice, fabricates intimacy, or mechanically bans sentence shapes.

## System coherence

- 2: Terminology, actions, hierarchy, state messages, and adjacent copy form one system without redundant echoes.
- 1: Mostly coherent with one material inconsistency.
- 0: Local edits create conflicting vocabulary, actions, or claims.

## English and Mexican Spanish

- 2: Meaning, confidence, action consequence, terminology, register, and claim strength remain equivalent; `es-MX` uses owned locale evidence and rendered review, with unresolved approval stated.
- 1: Accurate but literal, weakly localized, or missing one layout/format check.
- 0: Translation changes the offer, guarantee, action, or invents regional familiarity.
